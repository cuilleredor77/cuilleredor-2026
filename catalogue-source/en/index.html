14 · Organization

Prepare
your event.

A beautiful event is prepared weeks in advance. These steps let us cook, deliver and serve with peace of mind. The earlier you plan them, the smoother your big day will be.

1

Your venue

  • Access for professional vehicles
  • A preparation area or pantry
  • A water point and a 220 V power supply
  • A spot for the warming cabinet, if needed

Why is it important? We cook, plate and serve on site: without this equipment, the service cannot run as planned. The warming cabinet in particular keeps dishes at the right temperature until service: it is what guarantees hot food and consistent quality, even for a large number of guests. Check 60 days before, with the venue manager. If your venue keeps the food waste, let us know: the option (€50) is removed from the quote.

2

Travel

€1.90 per kilometre driven, outward and return journeys counted (e.g. venue 40 km away = 80 km × €1.90 = €152), from Vert-Saint-Denis, tolls and parking included, on a separate line of the quote. Beyond 250 km, accommodation costs are added, to be agreed with chef Huguette.

Why is it important? The venue determines the journey, the set-up time and the team needed. Give us the exact address as soon as you request your quote.

3 · The calendar, from booking to the big day

D − 60Balance and security depositThe guest count is frozen.We order the produce and book the team.
D − 30Late additionsLast confirmation of additional guests.The basis of our quantities and organization.
D − 14Menus, allergies, timesEverything is confirmed in writing.Allergy information protects your guests: report it without delay.
Event dayPossible extraIf there are more guests.We adjust the service live.

From D − 60, the guest count can no longer be reduced, only increased. Allergies and diets are taken into account as far as possible, without guarantee of a total absence of allergens. Beyond 30 minutes of overtime, €80 per hour started and per server.

4

The big day schedule

  1. ArrivalWelcoming guests, canapé reception
  2. MealBuffet, platter or plated service
  3. EveningWedding cake, entertainment, speeches, dance

Why is it important? A precise program lets the kitchen and service follow your rhythm. Send it to us 4 to 6 weeks before; times are confirmed 14 days before.

+

The tasting · €70 per person

The best way to choose with confidence. Optional but recommended, not deductible from the quote. By reservation, in a shared session of 2 to 10 people (1 h to 1 h 30), at the restaurant Chez Lina in Brunoy, between 3 pm and 6 pm or after 9 pm. Cancellation 48 h before.

Menu: chef’s appetizer (5 pieces) then a meal of your choice, buffet or platter service (2 meats, 2 fish, 1 vegetable, 3 sides) or plated (2 starters, 1 meat, 1 fish, 1 dessert).

16 · Chez Lina · Brunoy

A mother, four daughters, a legacy.

Mama Lina was born in Congo. She passed on to her four daughters what mattered most to her: attention to others, care given to every dish, and the idea that a table always brings together more than a meal.

Today, chef Huguette and her sisters keep this legacy alive in Brunoy, with Chez Lina: a Franco-African fusion restaurant, opened in tribute to their mother.

“Her name became our restaurant’s name. Her way of bringing people together became our way of hosting.”

The earthCassava, plantain and spices recall the family’s roots.
The fireThe embers give the dishes their slow cooking and their character.
The tableThe meal brings generations together and lets memories flow.
